How to Acquire the Alcaz Operative Disguise Toy: Quests and Locations - WoW Forever
By WoWSecrets
Updated: 2026/11/11
The  Alcaz Operative Insignia is a secret toy in World of Warcraft: Forever that gives you a Defias disguise. It starts in the new Alcaz Prison dungeon and takes you to Westfall, then to either Booty Bay or Stormwind depending on your faction. All the information for this secret has come from The WoW Secret Finding Discord.



 
Before and After - Credit: @Aluuni




Thank you to The WoW Secret Finding Discord for providing the information in this guide. Special thanks to @Miko @Hobe, and @Aluuni for writing the guide and testing it. If you think secret finding is for you, you can join the hunt in Discord at the link below.




WoW Secret Finding Discord


ALCAZ OPERATIVE DISGUISE TOY IN WOW: FOREVER
The Alcaz Operative Disguise is a toy that transforms you into a member of the Defias. To build it, you need six Fractured Insignia. You earn one by completing this quest chain, and the other five can be bought or traded from other players.

The Alcaz Operative Disguise toy
STEP 1: FREE MASON CONNERY IN ALCAZ PRISON
When you run the Alcaz Prison dungeon, do not kill the first boss, The Questioner. Skip past him and clear the rest of the dungeon.

At the end of the dungeon, a prisoner named Mason Connery offers a quest to go back, kill The Questioner, and bring him the key to his freedom, promising riches in return.

Kill The Questioner, loot the key, and bring it back to Mason Connery to complete the quest.

Mason Connery at the end of Alcaz Prison
STEP 2: MORE VALUABLE THAN GOLD IN WESTFALL
Mason Connery then gives you the quest More Valuable than Gold, along with a note that reads "WF, 43.4, 67.8". These coordinates lead to the inn at Moonbrook in Westfall.

Go to the second floor of the inn and find the box with interactable floorboards. Turn in the quest there to receive a Document Box.


Warning: the Document Box vendors for 25 gold. Do not sell it! You need it for the next step.

STEP 3: STATE SECRETS IN WOW: FOREVER
This step differs by faction. Both NPCs offer the same quest, State Secrets, which rewards a Fractured Insignia, some gold and experience.
Alliance: Stormwind
 RENZIK "THE SHIV"
Bring the Document Box to Renzik "The Shiv" in the SI:7 building in Old Town, Stormwind City, and accept State Secrets.

Horde: Booty Bay
 RIX XIZZIX
Bring the Document Box to Rix Xizzix in Booty Bay, who is near the Auction House, and accept State Secrets.

STEP 4: COMBINE THE FRACTURED INSIGNIA
The  Fractured Insignia is tradeable, so you do not need to run the chain six times. Buy the other five from other players, or trade them with friends.

Once you have six, combine them to create the Alcaz Operative Disguise toy.




Alcaz Operative Insignia
Item Level 1
Binds when picked up
Toy
Use: Disguise yourself as a Defias operative from Alcaz Island for 10 min. (30 Min Cooldown)
